Process capability

Tolerance

Routine work holds ±0.025 mm. The two DMG cells hold ±0.008 mm on bores and ±0.013 mm on profile in a temperature-controlled bay.

Surface finish

As-machined 1.6 µm Ra typical, 0.4 µm Ra achievable on flats with a fly cutter and finish pass.

Setup reduction

Five-axis trunnion work takes most parts to a single setup, which is where the repeatability on multi-feature bodies comes from.

Probing

Renishaw spindle probes on every center. Part zero is found in cycle, not on the setup sheet, so drift between lots is caught immediately.

Tool management

Presetter measured offsets loaded straight to the control. Broken-tool detection on unattended second and third shift running.

Coolant and chips

Through-spindle coolant at 70 bar on the deep-pocket machines. Chips are segregated by alloy for recycling credit.

Programming and files we accept

Mastercam 2025 with in-house post processors for each control. Send solids as STEP or Parasolid, drawings as PDF with GD&T called out. Native SolidWorks and Inventor files are fine too.
