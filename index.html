How a campaign runs

  1. Pick the audience

    Upload a list, connect your CRM, draw a radius around a location, or let a trigger build the audience for you as events arrive.

  2. Design the piece

    Start from a template and let AI draft the copy and layout. Every element can carry a merge field, so no two pieces have to be identical.

  3. Set the rules

    Monthly send caps, suppression windows, and holdout groups are configured once and then enforced on every send automatically.

  4. Track the response

    QR codes, personalized URLs, and call tracking tie every response back to the piece that caused it, so ROI is measured and not guessed.
